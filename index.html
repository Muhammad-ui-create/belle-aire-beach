Our Story

Good Food &
Great People

Located in the heart of the Monto Reno Marina on beautiful Lake Simcoe, Belle Aire Beach Bar & Grill is more than a restaurant — it's a gathering place for the community.

We serve homemade meals made with fresh ingredients and a whole lot of love. Whether you're pulling up by boat or driving down Killarney Beach Road, you'll feel right at home.

The Experience

Why Belle Aire

Lakeside Views

Dine with stunning views of Lake Simcoe. Watch the sunset paint the sky while enjoying your meal on our outdoor patio at the marina.

Homemade Goodness

Every dish is made from scratch with fresh, quality ingredients. From our juicy burgers to our crispy wings, you can taste the difference.

Community First
