Our Story

Good Food &
Great People

Located in the heart of the Monto Reno Marina on beautiful Lake Simcoe, Belle Aire Beach Bar & Grill is more than a restaurant — it's a gathering place for the community.

We serve homemade meals made with fresh ingredients and a whole lot of love. Whether you're pulling up by boat or driving down Killarney Beach Road, you'll feel right at home.
