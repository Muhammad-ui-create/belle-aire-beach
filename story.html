Since Day One

Our Story

How a love of food and community built Innisfil's lakeside gathering place.

⚓
The Beginning

A Dream by the Water

Belle Aire Beach Bar & Grill was born from a simple idea: create a place where people could enjoy great homemade food with an even greater view. Nestled in the Monto Reno Marina on the shores of Lake Simcoe, what started as a dream quickly became Innisfil's favourite gathering spot.

The founders saw something special in this stretch of Killarney Beach Road — a marina full of life, a lake full of beauty, and a community hungry for a place to call their own.

"We wanted to build a place where everyone feels like family — where the food is honest, the views are beautiful, and nobody's in a rush to leave."

🍔
The Kitchen

Homemade, Always

From the very first day, the kitchen at Belle Aire Beach has run on one principle: if it's not made with love, it doesn't leave the kitchen. Every burger is hand-formed, every sauce is house-made, and every plate goes out with pride.

The menu draws from classic comfort food — juicy burgers, crispy wings, golden fish and chips — but everything is elevated with fresh ingredients and real care. It's the kind of food that brings people back week after week.

Wing Night Thursdays became an instant tradition, and the signature Belle Aire Burger has earned a reputation that extends well beyond Innisfil.

🌅
The Setting

Life on the Lake

There's something about dining by the water that makes everything taste better. At Belle Aire Beach, your table comes with a front-row seat to Lake Simcoe's legendary sunsets, the gentle sound of boats at the dock, and the warm breeze off the water.

Whether you're arriving by car, by bike, or pulling up in your boat at the Monto Reno Marina, the atmosphere is relaxed, welcoming, and unmistakably lakeside. Outdoor seating lets you soak it all in, and even on the busiest summer evenings, there's a laid-back energy that keeps people smiling.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
The Community

Feeding Innisfil Like Family

Belle Aire Beach isn't just a restaurant — it's a community hub. The regulars know each other by name. Families celebrate milestones here. Friends reconnect over plates of nachos and cold drinks. It's the kind of place that turns first-time visitors into lifelong fans.

With 100% recommendation from reviewers and a growing family of loyal guests, the restaurant has become a staple of summer life in Innisfil. But to the team behind Belle Aire Beach, every season is a chance to welcome more people to the table.

"We're proud to feed the community like family. That's what it's always been about — good food, great people, and the best view in town."
